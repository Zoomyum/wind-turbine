# Wind Turbine Project
Wind turbine explained in here:
Miro board:
https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVHACUlGk=/?share_link_id=725397130524

This project is a small DIY wind turbine designed to generate and store electricity using wind.

The turbine uses a 1 metre diameter rotor with three 50 cm blades made from a 90 mm PVC stormwater pipe. The blades are spaced 120° apart and attached to a 20 cm × 20 cm 3D printed PETG hub.

The hub connects to a 12V 2000RPM, 25W PMDC motor using a 6 mm to 12 mm shaft coupler. The PMDC motor is used as a generator, so when the wind spins the blades, it spins the motor and produces electricity.

The generated electricity goes through a Schottky diode and into the charge controller. The controller charges a 12V 5Ah lead-acid battery, which stores the generated electricity. A 10A fuse is placed on the positive battery wire for protection. The charge controller also has USB ports that can be used to power small devices.

The turbine is mounted on a 100 × 100 mm × 3.6 m H4 treated pine post. Bearings are used in the rotating assembly to allow the rotor and shaft to spin smoothly.

The electronics will be kept inside a plastic enclosure to protect them from rain. The enclosure will have shade around it to reduce heating from direct sunlight, and silicone sealant will be used around suitable openings to help keep water out. The motor will also be protected from the weather while allowing the shaft to stick out and connect to the rotor.

All 3D printed parts are made using PETG.
